Meet Tooba

Tooba Connects is built around one simple idea: people thrive when they feel seen, heard and connected. Through speaking, workshops and community events, Tooba creates welcoming spaces where people can engage with important conversations around well-being, resilience, connection and personal growth.

Her approach is warm, relatable and people-centred, with sessions shaped around the audience in the room rather than a one-size-fits-all format. Whether the setting is a community gathering, organisational event or focused workshop, the aim is always the same: meaningful participation and lasting impact.
